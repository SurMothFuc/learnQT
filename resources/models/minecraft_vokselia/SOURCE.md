Original OBJ, MTL and PNG are preserved without modification. The learnQT scene adjusts PBR materials (white texture multiplier, rough diffuse blocks, alpha cutoff), selects nearest texture filtering and hard normals, and adds cameras and the existing project asset `resources/imported/glslpt/assets/HDR/white_cliff_top_1k.hdr` ([White Cliff Top, Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_cliff_top), CC0).

This is a static renderable scene, not a Minecraft game save. Geometry is grouped into 99 block-material meshes, not individually editable blocks. Water uses the pixel atlas and alpha cutoff rather than refractive water.
